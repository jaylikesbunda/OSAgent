New to OSAgent? Work through these pages in order:

1. **[Install]({{< relref "installation.md" >}})** — download the installer for your OS.
2. **[Quick start]({{< relref "quick-start.md" >}})** — pick a provider and run your first task.
3. **[Configuration]({{< relref "configuration.md" >}})** — providers, workspaces, tools, and permissions.

After that, connect a [model provider]({{< relref "../models/providers.md" >}}),
try the [web UI]({{< relref "../interfaces/web-ui.md" >}}), and browse the
[tools catalogue]({{< relref "../tools/overview.md" >}}).
