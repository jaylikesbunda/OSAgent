OSA ships three ways to talk to the same agent:

1. **[Web UI]({{< relref "web-ui.md" >}})** — embedded chat at `http://localhost:8765`.
2. **[Discord]({{< relref "discord.md" >}})** — per-channel sessions for servers and DMs.
3. **[Voice]({{< relref "voice.md" >}})** — Whisper speech-to-text plus Piper text-to-speech.
